I am having lots of issues exporting modpacks with Modrinth App due to having to resolve “datapacks and resourcepacks from outside the instance”, unable to duplicate instances, and their update all preview button feature is broken. This has slowed down my workflow and am considering swapping to Prism Launcher. Modrinth App won’t accept exporting datapacks and resourcepacks in .zip form so they have to be in folders.

There are now public teams (similar to Terraria). Use ``/corecraft team <team>`` to join a team. Benefits of being in a team includes team messaging command, friendly fire disabled, sharing waypoints, minimap and world map head icon sharing, and xp sharing for both the experience bar and skill tree.

Quests currently in beta. Issues from December 2025 still persist, but at least Odyssey Quests is now public on Modrinth. I disconnect from the server (server custom:payload errors?) when I try to set quests with item components such as mending enchanted books, so I haven’t got to set rewards. Some description elements are not working properly, so I haven’t created quest descriptions yet. Odyssey Allies also crashes.
